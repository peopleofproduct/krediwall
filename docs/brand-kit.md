# Brand kit — "Secured by Krediwall"

How adopters cite Krediwall, and how we may cite them. Two independent,
opt-in agreements in one document: the **badge licence** (we grant, you
display) and the **press-reference option** (you grant, we display).
Neither is implied by adoption, and neither obliges anyone.

> These terms should receive a counsel review before enforcement.
> The maintainers drafted them to be fair and conservative; they are
> not legal advice.

---

## 1. The badge licence (Krediwall → adopter)

If your organisation runs the Krediwall engine in production for a
stated scope (e.g. "credit-funded UPI payments"), you may display the
**"Secured by Krediwall"** badge on your security, trust or compliance
pages to state that fact, subject to:

- **Accuracy.** The badge must describe what actually runs. If the
  deployment stops, the badge comes down with it. Scope wording must be
  accurate — don't imply coverage of products the engine doesn't screen.
- **No distortion.** Use the provided artwork (a simple SVG, neutral
  wording — *additional fraud screening on credit-funded UPI*); no
  recolouring, animation or incorporation into your own logo.
- **No implied endorsement.** The badge states that you use the
  software — not that the maintainers endorse, certify or partner with
  you. (Paid-tier customers who complete the **Krediwall Verified
  Deployment** review may use the corresponding "Verified" wording.)
- **Revocation.** We may ask you to remove the badge for breaches of
  these terms or inaccurate claims; you may remove it at any time for
  any reason.
- **No trademark grant back.** Displaying the badge gives you rights in
  our names for the badge only — nothing broader, and it puts no
  obligations on you.

## 2. The press-reference option (adopter → Krediwall, opt-in)

Separate and optional: you may authorise us to name you as a user of
Krediwall in our own materials — this list, the README, conference
talks, the annual report. If you opt in:

- You choose the level each time or once: *name only*, *name + logo*,
  or *named case study* (case-study text is pre-approved by you before
  publication).
- **Joint disclosure review.** Every public mention that names you is
  sent to your named contact for a courtesy review before it goes out.
  You may veto any specific mention.
- **30-day revocability.** Withdraw the authorisation and we stop new
  uses within 30 days and remove existing ones within a reasonable
  migration window.
- No exclusivity, no fees, no obligation on either side.

Opting in is usually done by a short exchange with your marketing or
compliance contact — email confirmation is sufficient; your legal team
can escalate it to a signature if they prefer.

## 3. What we never do

- We never publish that an organisation uses Krediwall without opt-in
  at the chosen level — not in decks, not in the ADOPTERS.md file, not
  in the annual report.
- We never use your logo where the implication could read as
  endorsement, investment or partnership rather than "runs the
  software".
- We never track adoption in the product. The engine logs nothing and
  makes no outbound calls (see [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md)); any
  partner list comes from people telling us, not software phoning home.
