# Adopters

Organisations running Krediwall in production. Listing is **voluntary,
opt-in, and revocable at any time** — Apache-2.0 requires no
disclosure, and we don't track usage (the engine makes no outbound
calls, ever).

If you use Krediwall, we'd love to list you. Open a PR editing the
table below, or email the maintainers, at whichever disclosure level
you're comfortable with:

| Level | What we may show |
|---|---|
| **Name only** | Your organisation's name in this list |
| **Name + logo** | Name and logo here and in "trusted by" contexts |
| **Case study** | A named reference and write-up, approved by you before publication |

## Why list yourself

Your security posture is part of your product. A public listing is an
easy, credible way to show regulators, partners and customers that you
run additional pre-authorisation fraud screening on credit-funded UPI.
Organisations that want a citable credential can also apply for the
**Krediwall Verified Deployment** review (commercial tier) and display
the ["Secured by Krediwall" badge](docs/brand-kit.md) under its usage
guidelines.
